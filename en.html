Introduction

Chuancheng Song, Ph.D. student at Information Engineering Institute, CAS, supervised by Researcher Yanan Cao (National Young Talent). His research focuses on graph foundation models, graph anomaly detection, information content security, and complex network analysis. Selected for the CAS Youth Talent Program, he addresses national cybersecurity challenges with trustworthy graph intelligence. He has published multiple CCF-A and SCI papers and served as PC for AAAI, ICDM, COLING, ICIC, and as the first teaching assistant for the Academician Fang Binxing Class. Awards include provincial excellent graduate, Ph.D. first-class scholarship, full scholarship for Stanford University visit, etc.
