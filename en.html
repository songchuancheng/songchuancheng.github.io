Introduction

I am currently a Ph.D. student at the Information Engineering Institute, Chinese Academy of Sciences, supervised by Prof. Yanan Cao (National Young Talent). My research focuses on graph foundation models, graph anomaly detection, information security, and complex network analysis. I am selected as the Young Talent Fellow (Ph.D.) of the China Association for Science and Technology for my research efforts in trustworthy graph intelligence. I have published several papers in top-tier journals and conferences and have served as a PC member for AAAI, ICDM, COLING, ICIC, etc. I am also the first teaching assistant of the Fang Binxing Academician Experimental Class.
